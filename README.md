# Vrinda Store Sales Analysis 📊

An Excel-based sales and customer analysis project built using Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data.

The project focuses on understanding sales performance, customer demographics, order status, top-performing states, and sales channels through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data to identify important patterns and business insights.

The analysis covers:

- Monthly sales and order performance
- Customer distribution by gender
- Order status
- Top 5 states by sales
- Age group and gender comparison
- Sales performance across different channels

The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to present the analysis in a simple and easy-to-understand way.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How do sales and orders change throughout the year?
- What is the distribution of men and women customers?
- What is the overall order status distribution?
- Which states generate the highest sales?
- Which age groups contribute more to orders?
- How do different sales channels perform?

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Orders vs Sales
Monthly comparison of orders and sales to understand sales trends throughout 2022.

### 2. Men vs Women
Analysis of customer distribution based on gender.

### 3. Order Status
Breakdown of orders based on their current status, including Delivered, Cancelled, Refunded and Returned.

### 4. Top 5 States
Identifies the top-performing states based on total sales.

### 5. Age Group vs Gender
Comparison of customer/order contribution across Adult, Senior and Teenager age groups.

### 6. Sales Channels
Analysis of sales contribution from channels such as Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio and others.
